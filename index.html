FAQ

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. BeautyOS has no account and no server of its own.

How do I restore Premium? Profile › Restore purchases, or Restore Purchases on the Premium screen.

How do I cancel? Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Free features and your data stay available.

Is this medical advice? No. Insights are general information from fixed, sourced rules. Ask a dermatologist about skin conditions, pregnancy or prescription treatments.
