Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (v1.1)

BeautyOS does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your products, dates, photos, ingredient lists, routines and check-offs are stored only on your iPhone. You can export them (Profile › Export) or delete them (Profile › Delete all data) at any time. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or photograph a label. Text is recognised on the device; images are never uploaded.

Product lookups

BeautyOS includes an offline copy of the Open Beauty Facts product database, so many searches never leave your iPhone. When you scan a barcode or search by name, the barcode or search words may also be sent to these open product databases to fetch product details: Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts and Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org family), and, only if those don't know a barcode, UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com). No identifier about you is sent, and BeautyOS doesn't keep a record of your lookups.

Backups and crash reports

Backups you create are files you save or share yourself. iOS may collect crash details on your iPhone; they are only sent if you choose to share them from Profile.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone.

Children

BeautyOS is not directed at children under 13.
